Four Worlds · יהוה

The Geometry of the Name

Geometric proof

The Name unfolds through four worlds as one point in different positions.

Yod, the Father, is the point, set at the head of Atziluth. The first Heh, the Mother, receives it in Briah and reflects it to the center: the Son, the Father's image, born at the midpoint of the whole. Vav extends that image as a line through Yetzirah. The final Heh, the Daughter, reflects it once more to the lower edge, where it rests in Assiah.

The last point is the first, after another manner.
