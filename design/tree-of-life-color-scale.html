The wheel

The scale is one structure: twelve hues around the wheel for the twelve simples, the three primaries for the three mothers, and the seven doubles taking the primaries and the four remaining points of the wheel. Learning the colour is learning the attribution, which is why an arbitrary palette, however tasteful, teaches nothing.
