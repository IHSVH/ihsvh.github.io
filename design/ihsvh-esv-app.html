Tiphareth

THE TREE OF LIFE

The Center That Is Not Self-Grounded

6Tiphareth תפארת receives from Kether by the direct path aand distributes to Malkuth by the same. 2Its function on the map is mediation: every pillar crosses here, band nothing passes between the supernals and the lower seven without this transit. 3The sphere is named for harmony1 because harmony is what mediation produces when it is faithful.

4The sun is not the source of its own light in this system; cit is the first place light becomes visible. 5Case reads the solar attribution as functional, not astronomical: the sphere is solar because it does for the Tree what the sun does for a body.

Practice

6Sit. Locate the sternum. 7Breathe until the breath arrives there without effort, then hold the name יהוה אלוה ודעת silently through one exhale.

Start a Practice

Work the sphere for seven days: one reading, one sitting, one line of witness each morning.
