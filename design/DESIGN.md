# IHSVH design

Two artifacts define the house style. When they disagree, the ESV-app treatment is current.

- `ihsvh-html-styling-guide.html` — the full system: voice hierarchy, block grammar, section structure. Its EB Garamond body face is superseded by Crimson Pro.
- `ihsvh-esv-app.html` — reference treatment (Sep 2026), approved. Use for any new document or app.

## Tokens (ESV-app treatment)

paper   #F4F1E4   card #FBF9F2   ink #46453F   rule #E1DBC9
gold    #B4A67F (bar)   gold-deep #AC9D71 (button)   line #BFB18F   ref #72ABBF

Treatment: sticky gold bar with white sans chapter name; centered line-drawn plate; small-cap title; italic section heads; hung light chapter numeral; verse numbers in ink; cross-refs italic in the blue; body ~1.32rem at 1.72 line-height; white card with gold button; hairline rows with blue outline icons and chevrons.
