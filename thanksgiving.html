Timeline

Times follow the dinner time above. Use a thermometer, not the clock, to decide when the turkey is done.

Turkey temp: pull at 155°F in the thickest part of the breast; it climbs while resting. USDA's safety guideline is 165°F, so cook to that if you prefer. Thighs should read 170°F or more.

Recipes

Turkey rub

Butterball is pre-brined, so use little salt or none.

  • 3 tbsp soft butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp chopped thyme and sage
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp black pepper, ½ tsp salt at most
  • 1 tsp baking powder (gluten-free label), dusted on the skin

Butter goes under the skin, not on top, so the skin stays crisp. Tuck the wing tips behind the breast so they don't burn.

Gravy stock

  • Backbone and neck (if Hy-Vee packed them)
  • ½ onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery rib
  • 4 cups water, or gluten-free broth for more flavor

Brown the bones and vegetables, add liquid, simmer 90 minutes, strain. No bones? Use 3 cups gluten-free broth.

Gravy

  • 2 tbsp turkey fat + 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch whisked into ¼ cup cold stock
  • 3 cups warm stock + pan drippings
  • 1 tsp cider vinegar, salt to taste

Bring the warm stock, drippings, fat and butter to a simmer. Whisk in the cornstarch slurry and simmer 2–3 minutes until thick. Never add dry cornstarch to hot liquid.

Wild rice pilaf

  • 1 cup Canoe wild rice, cooked in 3 cups broth
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion and 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 2 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp thyme, 1 tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup plain Original Craisins
  • ½ cup toasted pepitas, ¼ cup parsley, 2 tsp cider vinegar (add at the end)

Toast the dry rice in a little butter for 1–2 minutes, add the broth, cover and simmer 45–55 minutes until the grains split open; drain any extra liquid. Separately, brown the mushrooms in butter without stirring, add the onion, celery, thyme and salt and cook until soft, then fold in the rice and Craisins.

Mashed potatoes

  • 1½ lb bite-size gold potatoes (Cal-Organic, 24 oz), whole, skins on
  • 4 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • ½–¾ cup heavy cream, warmed
  • 1 tbsp salt for the water, more to taste

Cover the potatoes and garlic with cold water, add the salt, and bring to a boil. Simmer 12–15 minutes until a knife slides in. The skins are thin, so leave them on. Drain, return to the hot pot for 1 minute to dry, then mash by hand with the garlic, butter and warm cream. Never use a mixer or blender; it turns them gluey. Season and keep covered.

Cranberry sauce

  • 12 oz fresh cranberries
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup orange juice and ¼ cup water
  • Zest of ½ orange, pinch of salt

Zest the orange before juicing it. Bring the juice, water and sugar to a boil, add the cranberries, zest and salt, and simmer about 10 minutes, until most berries pop. It thickens as it cools. Serve cold or at room temperature.

Brussels sprouts

  • 12 oz sprouts, halved
  • 2 tbsp oil, ½ tsp salt
  • Lemon zest and juice, 1 tsp maple syrup

Spread in one layer with space between them, cut side down. Roast at 450°F for about 20 minutes, flipping once halfway, then toss with the lemon and maple.
